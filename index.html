What Is a Sound Bath?

You lie down, get comfortable and listen while singing bowls are played around you. There's nothing to do and nothing to get right. The long, fading tones give your attention one simple thing to follow, and many people find it the easiest way into meditation.

  • A quiet hour to slow down and unwind
  • A calm way to settle the mind before sleep
  • A focus point that makes meditation easier for beginners
  • Gentle enough for anyone, with no experience needed

Sound baths are a relaxation and meditation practice. They are not a medical treatment and don't replace care from a doctor or mental health professional.

About

Singing Bowl Australia is run by Prakash Bhattarai, a meditation teacher based in the Canberra region. Prakash runs the Meditation for Beginners community and makes the daily Calm in 60 stress-relief videos.

Singing bowls have been part of Himalayan practice for generations. Here they're used for what they do best: helping ordinary, busy people stop for a moment, listen and come back to themselves.
