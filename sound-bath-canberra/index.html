Sound Baths in Canberra & Queanbeyan

Singing bowl sound baths and meditation sessions with Prakash Bhattarai: one-on-one, small groups, or online from anywhere.

By Prakash Bhattarai · 14 November 2026 · 2 min read

If you live in Canberra or Queanbeyan and want to try a sound bath, you can book one with me. I'm Prakash Bhattarai, a meditation teacher based in the Canberra region, and I use singing bowls to help busy people slow down and rest. Sessions are arranged on request, so tell me what suits you and I'll email you times and prices.

Request a session →

Session Options

One-on-one sound bath

A private session at a time that suits you. You lie down and rest while I play singing bowls around you, with a little guided breathing at the start and quiet time at the end. Good for a first experience, or if you'd rather not be in a group.

Small group sound bath

For friends, families, workplaces, yoga groups or community events in the Canberra region. Everyone lies down together; there's nothing to learn or do.

Online session

Not in Canberra? Join from anywhere over video. You'll lie down at home with headphones while I play and guide. Many people find this surprisingly relaxing, and there's no drive home afterwards.

Areas We Cover

In-person sessions are available across Canberra and nearby, including Queanbeyan, Jerrabomberra, Belconnen, Gungahlin, Woden, Weston Creek, Tuggeranong and the inner north and south. Let me know your suburb when you request a session.

What to Expect

  1. We agree on a time and place by email.
  2. You settle in lying on a mat with a pillow and blanket.
  3. The sound: singing bowls played slowly, letting each tone ring and fade.
  4. Quiet time at the end, then a few minutes to come back before you go.

New to sound baths? Read what is a sound bath for a full explanation and how to prepare.

Sound baths are a relaxation and meditation practice, not a medical treatment. Please tell me about any health concerns (for example pregnancy, epilepsy, a pacemaker or sound sensitivity) before we start, and check with your doctor if you're unsure.

Try It at Home First

Want a taste before you book? Lie down with headphones and play the free singing bowl loop, which is my own bowl, for 10 or 20 minutes. Or follow the 10-minute singing bowl meditation.

PRACTISE WITH OTHERS

Why a Community Makes This Easier

Most people don't stop meditating because it doesn't work. They stop because life gets busy and nobody notices. A group changes that: you sit at the same time as others, you can ask questions when something feels strange, and you see beginners just like you keep going.

Prakash runs the Meditation for Beginners community: short daily practices, live sessions and a friendly place to ask anything. Bring your singing bowl, or just yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a sound bath cost?

It depends on the type of session, group size and location. Send a request and I'll email you prices and available times.

Do I need to bring anything?

Comfortable warm clothes and socks. For group sessions, let me know if you need a mat and blanket.

Can you run a sound bath for my workplace or event?

Yes. Small group sessions can be arranged for workplaces, schools, yoga studios and community groups around Canberra. Mention it in your request.
