Why a Community Makes This Easier

Most people don't stop meditating because it doesn't work. They stop because life gets busy and nobody notices. A group changes that: you sit at the same time as others, you can ask questions when something feels strange, and you see beginners just like you keep going.

Prakash runs the Meditation for Beginners community: short daily practices, live sessions and a friendly place to ask anything. Bring your singing bowl, or just yourself.
